Cape Town Fortnightly

What's on in Cape Town and the Winelands.

Not listed: online-only events, conferences and trade shows, religious services.

Each event links to the organiser or ticket seller. Times and prices change, so check there before you go.

Made in Cape Town by Timothy de Wet. The listings are gathered every week by software from venues' and organisers' own announcements, their newsletters and Instagram, and the ticket sites, then read and sorted by a language model, so mistakes happen. Report a mistake.

Visits are counted without cookies or personal data. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
